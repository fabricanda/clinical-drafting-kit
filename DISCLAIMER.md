# Disclaimer

1. **Drafting aid only.** Output is a draft for review by qualified professionals. It is not medical, regulatory or legal advice.
2. **Not validated.** The kit has not been validated for GxP use. Sign-off is a JSON log, not a 21 CFR Part 11 electronic signature or audit trail.
3. **Your data, your model.** The kit sends study data only to the model provider you configure. Do not enter confidential or patient-identifiable data into any model or tier your organization has not approved.
4. **Check against current rules.** Regulations and guidance change. You are responsible for confirming every draft against the versions that apply to your study.
5. **Examples are fictional.** No company's confidential data is included.

Context: FDA's January 2025 draft guidance on AI to support regulatory decision-making for drugs and biologics, and EMA's 2024 reflection paper on AI in the medicinal product lifecycle, both tie expectations to the AI's context of use and influence on decisions. This kit drafts text for human review; your quality function decides how it may be used.
